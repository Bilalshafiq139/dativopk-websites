Twelve years of shaadiyan

Dastaan started in 2014 with one camera and a cousin's Mehndi in Model Town. Today we are a team of fourteen photographers, filmmakers and editors, and we still treat every family like our own.

Our story

Shaadi ek din ki, tasveer umar bhar ki

We grew up flipping through our parents' wedding albums: the faded colours, the stiff stage photos, and the one candid shot everyone loved. We wanted to make albums full of those candid shots.

So we plan every wedding with the family, not just the couple. We learn the names of the phuppos and the khalas, we know when each rasm happens and who has to be in the photo, and we stay out of the way when it matters.

Our studio on MM Alam Road is where families come to plan, pick albums and drink a lot of chai. Come visit.

Faiza Qureshi, founder

How it works

From first call to album

  1. Baat-cheet

    A call or studio visit to understand your events, family and style.

  2. Advance

    A written quote, then 30% advance to lock your dates.

  3. Planning

    Timeline, family photo lists and venue walk-through two weeks before.

  4. Shaadi

    The team covers every rasm. Sneak peeks within 48 hours.

  5. Album

    Gallery in 3 to 4 weeks, album and film soon after you approve.
