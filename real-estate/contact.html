Questions buyers ask

Short answers. Your agent will explain the rest over a cup of chai.

What is your commission?

1% from the buyer and 1% from the seller on a sale, and half a month's rent on a rental. No hidden charges.

Do you check the property papers?

Yes. Our legal team checks the title, society NOC, dues and any bank charge before you pay a token.

Can overseas Pakistanis buy through you?

Yes. We do video viewings, and a power of attorney lets a family member or our team complete the transfer for you.

Do you help with home loans or installments?

We work with major banks for home finance, and our new projects come with builder installment plans of up to 3 years.
