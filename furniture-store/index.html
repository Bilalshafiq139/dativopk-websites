We make fifteen pieces and nothing else. Each one in oak, sheesham, walnut or ebonised ash, sized for real rooms and built to be handed down.

Lakri means wood. Our workshop sits in Chiniot, a town that has carved furniture for three hundred years. Ten carpenters, one finisher, and no factory line.
