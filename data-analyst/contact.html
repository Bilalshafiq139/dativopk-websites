FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

My data is messy. Is that a problem?

No. Almost every project starts with messy Excel files. The first week is a data health check, and I tell you honestly what can be used and what needs fixing.

Do I need to buy Power BI or other software?

Not always. I often start with what you already have (Excel, Google Sheets, Looker Studio). If a paid tool makes sense, I'll explain the monthly cost before we start.

How do you keep our data private?

I sign an NDA by default, work inside your systems where possible, and delete my copies at the end of the project.

How do payments work?

Fixed price per project: 40% to start, 40% at the dashboard or model review, 20% after handover. Monthly partner work is billed at the start of each month.

Can you train my team after the project?

Yes. Every project includes a handover session. Longer workshops in Urdu or English can be added for teams of up to 15 people.
