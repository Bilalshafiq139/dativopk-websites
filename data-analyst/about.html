I started in a retail head office, copying numbers between spreadsheets every Monday. I've spent every year since making sure no team has to do that again.

My work sits between the people who make decisions and the systems that hold the data. I ask a lot of questions before I write any SQL, because a beautiful dashboard that answers the wrong question is still the wrong dashboard.

Clients usually come to me with one of three problems: reports that take too long, numbers nobody trusts, or a big question ("why are we losing customers?") that nobody has had time to answer properly. I fix the first two with clean data models and live dashboards, and the third with focused analysis.

I teach in Urdu and English, and I leave every team able to maintain what I built. Outside work I mentor junior analysts in a Lahore data community and write about Pakistani market data.
