My story

I started with a borrowed camera and a lot of patience

My first camera belonged to my uncle. I shot my cousins’ weddings with it for free, mostly because nobody else would stand that close to the dhol.

Ten years later, the studio in DHA works with families, designers and small brands across Pakistan. The idea is still the same: get close, stay calm, and wait for the moment people forget the camera is there.

I shoot portraits and fashion in the studio and on the street, weddings across Pakistan, and product photos for brands that sell on Instagram.
