Good to know

Questions people ask before booking

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, three to six months is ideal, especially for December and the spring season. Portrait and product shoots can usually happen within one to two weeks.

Do you travel outside Lahore?

Yes. We shoot across Pakistan and abroad. Travel and stay are added at cost, and there is no extra fee for travel days inside Punjab.

How does payment work?

A 30% advance confirms your date. The rest is due on the shoot day for portraits and products, and before album delivery for weddings. Bank transfer, Easypaisa and JazzCash are all fine.

When will I get my photos?

Portraits in 7 days, product shoots in 3 to 5 days, and weddings in about 21 days. You will get a few sneak peeks within 48 hours.

Can I get the raw files?

We deliver fully edited, high-resolution photos only. Raw files are part of our process, not the final product, so we keep them archived for a year in case you need a re-edit.

Do you provide makeup and styling?

We work with trusted makeup artists and stylists in Lahore and can book them for you. Their fee is shared in your quote.
