Delivery & orders

Quick answers about delivery areas, payment and returns.

Where do you deliver drinks?

Across Lahore: DHA, Gulberg, Cantt, Model Town, Johar Town, Garden Town, Bahria Town and more. Drinks arrive in 30 to 50 minutes with ice packed on the side.

Do you ship outside Lahore?

Yes. Matcha powder, hojicha, kits and teaware ship to every city in Pakistan by courier in 2 to 4 working days.

How much is delivery?

Rs 250 per order, and free on orders over Rs 5,000.

How can I pay?

Cash on delivery, debit or credit card (secure link by SMS), Easypaisa or JazzCash.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Send us a photo on WhatsApp within 24 hours and we will replace it or refund you, no questions asked.
