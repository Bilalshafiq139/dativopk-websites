Your common questions

Everything about our matcha, how to make it, how to store it and how delivery works.

Is your matcha 100% pure?

Yes. Our matcha is 100% stone-ground Japanese green tea leaves with no added sugar, flavours, colours or fillers. Sweetness in our drinks comes only from what you choose.

Does matcha contain caffeine?

Yes, about 60 to 70 mg in a 2 g serving, a little less than a cup of coffee. Because it comes with L-theanine, the energy feels smoother and lasts longer.

How do I prepare matcha?

Sift 2 g (one bamboo scoop) into a bowl, add 70 ml of water at 70 to 80°C and whisk in a quick zig-zag for 20 seconds until foamy. Drink it straight or pour it over milk and ice. Our recipes page has step-by-step guides.

Can I drink matcha every day?

Most people enjoy one or two cups a day. If you are sensitive to caffeine or pregnant, talk to your doctor first, or try our low-caffeine hojicha.

Where do you deliver?

Drinks and treats are delivered across Lahore (DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria and more). Powder, kits and teaware ship to every city in Pakistan in 2 to 4 days with cash on delivery.
