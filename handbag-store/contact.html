Delivery & exchange

How long does delivery take?
We ship from Lahore within a day. Leopards or TCS deliver in 1 to 2 days in Lahore and 2 to 4 days elsewhere in Pakistan.
How much is delivery?
Rs 350 anywhere in Pakistan, free on orders over Rs 20,000.
Can I pay cash on delivery?
Yes, on every order. You can also pay by card, bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash.
Can I exchange a bag?
Within 7 days, if the bag is unused and has its tags. Message us on WhatsApp and we send a rider to collect it.
Do you do bulk or corporate orders?
Yes, from 20 bags, with your logo on a leather tag and custom gift boxes. Use the form above and choose "Bulk / corporate".
