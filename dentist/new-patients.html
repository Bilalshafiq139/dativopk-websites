Before you come

What to Bring

Your CNIC

We register every patient so your records, X-rays and plans stay safe and in one place.

Your medicines list

Some medicines, like blood thinners, change how we plan treatment. A photo of the packets is fine.

Old X-rays or reports

If you had dental work elsewhere, bring what you have. It helps us compare and avoid repeat X-rays.
