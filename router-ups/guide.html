Step zero

Check your router's label.

Turn the router over. Near the barcode you'll see its input. Match that voltage with Jugnu's output and you're done.

Fiber homes

Got two boxes?

PTCL Flash Fiber, Nayatel and StormFiber give you an ONT (the fiber box) plus a WiFi router. Both need power or the internet stops. Take the 20,000 mAh Jugnu: run the router from the 12V output and the fiber box from the 9V or second cable, if your ONT takes 9V, or ask us for a Y-cable.
