    Before you apply.

    Do I need any prior experience?

    No. Five of our six programmes start from zero. Machine Learning is easier if you know a little Python, and the first week includes a refresher.

    Classes are online and live on weekends, so they fit around a job or university. Every session is recorded in case you miss one.

    Yes. Students who complete the final project receive a verified certificate that can be added to LinkedIn.

    Can the fee be paid in instalments?

    Yes. The fee can be split into two monthly instalments. Our admissions team will explain the details on your call.

    Do you help with jobs or freelancing?

    Each programme ends with a portfolio review, CV and LinkedIn guidance, and practical advice on finding your first clients.
