Our story

Nexora began in 2021 with a simple frustration: talented people were paying for courses that taught theory, and then struggling to do the actual job.

So we built something different. Short programmes, each focused on one skill that employers and clients pay for today. Every class is taught by someone who does that work for a living, and every module ends with something you have actually built.

We keep batches small on purpose. Twenty students means your mentor knows your name, reads your work, and tells you honestly what to improve. Our graduates now work at agencies, startups and multinationals, and many run independent practices of their own.
