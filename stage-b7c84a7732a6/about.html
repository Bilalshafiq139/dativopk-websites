About DATIVOPK

A Pakistani online academy teaching practical data skills: Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI, live and step by step.

Who we are

Tech skills for a secure future

DATIVOPK is an online training academy based in Faisalabad. All classes are 100% online and live on Google Meet, so students join from anywhere in Pakistan.

We focus on what employers ask for: strong Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI, learned through real datasets and portfolio projects rather than theory alone. Every class is recorded, and mentors help on WhatsApp throughout the course.
