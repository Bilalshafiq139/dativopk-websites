Overview

About this course

Our new-format analytics course takes you through the three tools data analysts use every day: SQL to pull and combine data, Python to clean and analyse it, and Power BI to turn it into dashboards.

Classes are live on Google Meet three times a week, every class is recorded, and you build real portfolio projects along the way.

Questions

Course FAQ

Are the classes online or physical?

100% online, live on Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere. There are no physical classes.

I have a job. What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded and shared in your class group. You can learn from the recordings and still get the certificate, as long as you submit your assignments and projects on time.

How do I pay the fee?

By Meezan Bank or JazzCash. We share the details on WhatsApp; you send the fee slip in the same chat and receive the class group link once it's verified.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund within the first 3 classes if the course isn't right for you: timing, teacher or anything else.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of completion (soft copy) that you can add to LinkedIn. It is given when you submit your assignments and portfolio projects on time.

Do I need to buy software?

No. All software used in the course is free, and the mentor helps you install it on WhatsApp after registration.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Pay the whole course at once, or per month: month 1 before the course starts and month 2 before month 2 begins.
