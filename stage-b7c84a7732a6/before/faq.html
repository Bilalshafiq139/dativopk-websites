Classes

Are the classes online or physical?

100% online, live on Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere. There are no physical classes.

I have a job. What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded and shared in your class group. You can learn from the recordings and still get the certificate, as long as you submit your assignments and projects on time.

Can I join on a mobile phone?

A laptop or PC is needed for the classes and assignments. A phone alone is not enough.

Do I need to buy software?

No. All software used in the course is free, and the mentor helps you install it on WhatsApp after registration.

Courses

Which course should I start with?

New to Excel or computers: Advanced Excel. Comfortable with a computer and want SQL, Python and Power BI: Advanced Data Analytics. Excel basics help but are not required for it.

Is Excel part of the Data Analytics course?

No. Advanced Data Analytics covers SQL, Python and Power BI. Advanced Excel is a separate course with its own batch.

Are Macros/VBA or Tableau included?

No, they are not part of the current courses.

Dates & fees

When does the next batch start?

The dates on this website are always the latest. New batches and demo dates are also announced first in our WhatsApp group.

The batch has started. Can I still join?

Message us on WhatsApp. If you want to start right away, we will tell you what is possible for the running batch.

How do I pay the fee?

By Meezan Bank or JazzCash. We share the details on WhatsApp; you send the fee slip in the same chat and receive the class group link once it's verified.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund within the first 3 classes if the course isn't right for you: timing, teacher or anything else.

Are there any registration charges?

No. You only pay the course fee shown on the course page.

Is there a Free Demo Class?

Yes. The demo is free and the same for both courses. See the Schedule & Demo page for the next date.

Certificate

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of completion (soft copy) that you can add to LinkedIn. It is given when you submit your assignments and portfolio projects on time.
