Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

DATIVOPK (dativopk.com) is an online training academy in Pakistan offering live online courses in Excel and Data Analytics. This page explains how we handle your information when you contact us on WhatsApp or through our website.

What we collect

  • Your name and WhatsApp number.
  • The messages, voice notes, photos and files you send us (for example course questions, fee payment slips and assignments).
  • The course you are interested in or enrolled in, and your fee and class status.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions about our courses, fees and class timings.
  • To complete your admission, verify your fee and add you to your class group.
  • To check your assignments and send you class updates and reminders.

Automated replies

Some of our WhatsApp replies are written with the help of an AI assistant, supervised by our team. Your messages are processed by trusted service providers only to produce these replies.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services we need to run our classes: WhatsApp (Meta), our website hosting, and our AI service providers. We never share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a student or in contact with us, and for our records afterwards. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • Reply "stop" and we will not send you any more messages.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information.
